# My Favorite Series/Movies

I really enjoy series that have **unique stories**, *beautiful animation*, and deep emotions.  
Here are some of my favorites:

---

## 1. Look Back
**Type:** Short Anime Film  
> A touching story about friendship, art, and growing up.

## 2. Ping Pong the Animation
**Type:** Anime Series  
> A fast-paced and emotional story about talent and self-worth.

## 3. Mushishi
**Type:** Anime Series  
> Calm and relaxing. Each episode tells a quiet and meaningful story about nature and life.

## 4. Orb: On the Movements of the Earth
**Type:** Anime Film  
> A beautiful story that connects science, emotion, and discovery.

## 5. Bakemonogatari
**Type:** Anime Series  
> Known for its creative visuals and deep, strange dialogue.
